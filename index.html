About Us




Our mission is to serve the people of rural Nepal and bring them the Good News of Jesus Christ.




Nepal Darshan Church was founded in 2008 in
Kathmandu , Bagmati
- Jorpati 7 , Kathmandu ,Nepal

Darshan means "Vision" in Nepali.
