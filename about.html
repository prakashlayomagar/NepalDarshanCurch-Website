Our Story

How We Began

Nepal Darshan Church was founded in 2008 in Jorpati 7, Basigaum, Kathmandu, Bagmati, Nepal. "Darshan" means "Vision" in Nepali - a name chosen to reflect our calling to help the people of Nepal see and know the light of Jesus Christ.

What began as a small gathering of believers has grown into a church family committed to worship, discipleship, and service - reaching out to families, children, the elderly, and people with disabilities in Kathmandu and the surrounding rural villages.

Our Pastor

Pastor Shyam Magar has led Nepal Darshan Church since its founding, shepherding the congregation and traveling to rural villages to share the Gospel with communities who have never heard it or have no local church of their own.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve the people of rural Nepal and bring them the Good News of Jesus Christ - meeting practical needs while sharing the joy and hope found in the Lord.
