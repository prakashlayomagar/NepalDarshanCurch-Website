Support Our Ministry

Your prayers, gifts and time help us serve families, children, the elderly and people with disabilities across Kathmandu and rural Nepal. Here are a few ways you can support the ministry.

Give

To give financially toward the church and its outreach, please contact us by email or phone for current bank transfer and mobile payment details.

Pray

Pray for our congregation, for Pastor Shyam Magar, and for the villages we visit who have never heard the Gospel.

Volunteer

Join us on work projects and outreach trips to rural communities. Reach out to learn about upcoming opportunities.
